Code of Conduct

Our Pledge

As the maintainer of this project, I am committed to providing a respectful, welcoming, and harassment-free environment for everyone who wants to use, discuss, or contribute to this project.

All participants are expected to interact respectfully and professionally, regardless of their background, experience, identity, or personal characteristics.

Our Standards

Examples of behavior that contributes to a positive environment include:

* Being respectful and considerate toward others
* Demonstrating empathy and kindness
* Respecting different opinions, viewpoints, and experiences
* Giving and accepting constructive feedback
* Communicating in a professional and appropriate manner
* Taking responsibility for mistakes and learning from them
* Focusing on improving the project and helping other contributors

Examples of unacceptable behavior include:

* Harassment, discrimination, or personal attacks
* Insulting, trolling, or derogatory comments
* Use of sexualized language or imagery
* Unwelcome sexual attention or advances
* Publishing another person’s private information without permission
* Threatening, abusive, or intimidating behavior
* Any other conduct that could reasonably be considered inappropriate in a professional setting

Responsibilities

The project maintainer is responsible for clarifying and enforcing the standards described in this Code of Conduct.

The maintainer may remove, edit, or reject comments, issues, commits, pull requests, or other contributions that violate this Code of Conduct.

Appropriate action will be taken based on the nature and severity of the violation.

Scope

This Code of Conduct applies to all interactions related to this project, including:

* GitHub issues
* Pull requests
* Discussions
* Comments
* Project-related communication
* Other spaces where someone is representing or contributing to this project

Reporting

If you experience or witness abusive, harassing, threatening, or otherwise unacceptable behavior related to this project, please report it to the project maintainer through the contact information provided in the repository.

All reports will be reviewed and handled as fairly and confidentially as reasonably possible.

Enforcement

Depending on the nature and severity of a violation, the project maintainer may take one or more of the following actions:

1. Correction

For minor or unintentional violations, the contributor may receive a private warning and be asked to correct the behavior.

2. Warning

For repeated or more serious violations, the contributor may receive a formal warning and may be asked to stop the inappropriate behavior.

3. Temporary Restriction

For serious violations or continued inappropriate behavior, access to project discussions, issues, pull requests, or other project-related interactions may be temporarily restricted.

4. Permanent Restriction

For severe or repeated violations, the contributor may be permanently restricted from participating in project-related interactions.
